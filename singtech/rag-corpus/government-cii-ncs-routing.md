# Government and CII deals routed through NCS

Use this playbook when the customer is a government agency, a government contractor, or an owner of Critical Information Infrastructure (CII). Singapore's Cybersecurity Act sets obligations for CII owners in sectors including aviation, maritime, banking, and energy (source: https://www.csa.gov.sg/legislation/cybersecurity-act).

## When to route through NCS

Route the Opportunity as "NCS multi-LOB" when any of these is true:

- The scope includes application, cloud, or data work for a public-sector contract.
- The customer's contract terms require in-country processing or controls that a systems integrator must evidence.
- More than one NCS line of business is needed, for example Government and Public Sector plus Cyber Security Services.

NCS is Singtel Group's technology services arm for cloud migration, application modernisation, and data engineering, and it reported record FY26 bookings of S$3.8 billion (source: https://www.telecomtv.com/content/digital-platforms-services/singtel-posts-fy26-net-profit-of-s-5-61bn-underlying-profit-up-12-to-s-2-77bn-55535/).

## Roles

- Singtel account executive: owns the relationship, the connectivity, 5G+, and Cyber Security scope, and the Salesforce Opportunity.
- NCS client director: owns the NCS lines of business and chairs the programme board.
- Each NCS line of business is a separate line item on one Opportunity, so the customer sees one proposal.

## Motion

1. Confirm the CII or government scope in writing with the customer's contracts team.
2. Run feasibility for every site. An off-net site inside a CII scope needs an explicit SLA carve-out or a build plan.
3. Agree data residency early. Private 5G with multi-access edge computing keeps processing on site; RE:AI is hosted in Singapore.
4. Hold one joint programme board with NCS and record every decision in the Chatter deal room.
5. Map the paper process on day one; government-linked contracts often need a longer legal review.

## Watch-outs

- Never present Singtel and NCS as competing bidders on the same scope.
- Do not promise compliance outcomes. The customer remains the CII owner and is accountable for its obligations.
- Sample account Meridian Arcwell Group shows the full pattern: four NCS lines of business, an operations centre with data residency terms, and off-net overseas sites.
