# MEDDPICC qualification guide

SingTech scores each account's main Opportunity on the 8 MEDDPICC elements, 0 to 2 each, for a total of 0 to 16.

## Scoring

- 0: unknown or not started.
- 1: partly known, not yet confirmed by the customer.
- 2: confirmed by the customer, with evidence recorded.

## Elements

- Metrics: the measurable outcome the customer will judge success by, such as cutting a nightly sync from 5 hours to under 30 minutes.
- Economic Buyer: the person who can release budget. Score 2 only after direct contact.
- Decision Criteria: the written criteria and their weights.
- Decision Process: the steps, people, and dates from evaluation to signature.
- Paper Process: legal, procurement, PO, and supplier onboarding steps, including the billing system that will receive the invoice.
- Identify Pain: the business pain, with numbers and a source (telemetry, an incident, or the customer's own words).
- Champion: an insider who sells for us when we are not in the room. Test a champion by asking for something only an insider can provide.
- Competition: who else is in the deal, for which scope, and how the customer sees them.

## Forecast guide

This is a SingTech planning convention, not a Singtel standard.

- 12 to 16: commit candidate.
- 8 to 11: best case; close the named gaps.
- 0 to 7: pipeline only; qualify before investing specialist time.

## Rules

- Every score needs evidence text. Every score under 2 needs a gap and a next step.
- Paper Process scores 0 while an invoice is disputed or supplier onboarding is incomplete; finance goodwill matters.
- Re-score after every customer meeting and post the change in the Chatter deal room.
