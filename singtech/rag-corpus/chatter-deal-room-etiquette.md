# Chatter deal-room etiquette

Each Opportunity's Chatter feed in Salesforce is its deal room. SingTech posts drafts there; people approve them.

## Rules

1. One thread per decision or request. Start with a one-line ask, then the context.
2. Mention specialists by desk (Connectivity, Cyber, Digital InfraCo, NCS) instead of broadcasting to everyone.
3. After each customer meeting, post the MEDDPICC change: element, old score, new score, and evidence.
4. Every claim about the customer links its source. No source, no claim.
5. Keep personal data out. Refer to customer people by role, and never post personal phone numbers, personal emails, or ID numbers. Text bound for Slack or Salesforce passes the redaction step first.
6. Post pricing only as indicative figures derived from catalogue ARR or as an approved quote reference. Never post Singtel list pricing.
7. Put billing disputes in their own thread tagged with the invoice ID (for example SGT-INV-2026-8841), so finance can follow without reading the deal thread.
8. Approvals are explicit: write "Approved" and name the item. A reaction is not an approval.
9. Use plain enterprise language: no emojis and no exclamation marks.
10. Close the loop: when an action is done, reply in its thread with the outcome and link the Salesforce Task.

## Post template

- Ask: what you need, from which desk.
- Account and Opportunity: name, Stage, and indicative ARR.
- Evidence: the measured strain, incident, or customer quote, with its source.
- Decision needed by: date.
