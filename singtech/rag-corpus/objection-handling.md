# Objection handling

Acknowledge the concern, ask one clarifying question, answer with evidence, and confirm the next step. Never disparage a competitor and never quote competitor pricing without a source.

## "StarHub, M1, or NTT is cheaper"

- Ask what their quote covers: which sites, security controls, SLA terms, overseas coverage, and who manages partner tails.
- Compare total cost, including partner tails, separate security tooling, and the customer's in-house effort to run several vendors.
- Offer the starter bundle as a smaller first step instead of a discount. Pricing is indicative, derived from catalogue ARR.
- Lead with what one accountable partner removes: the incident hand-offs the customer already lived through.

## "Our data must stay in Singapore" or "in India"

- RE:AI is a sovereign AI cloud with GPU-as-a-Service and token-based AI services hosted in Singapore (source: https://www.re-ai.ai/about-us/newsroom/singtel-re-ai-first-in-APAC-to-deploy-nvidia-dgx-gb300).
- Nxera offers AI-ready colocation at the 58MW DC Tuas facility (source: https://www.itnews.asia/news/singtels-nxera-opens-58mw-data-centre-in-singapore-623491).
- Private 5G with multi-access edge computing keeps processing on site for CII and government scopes (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/5g).
- For India, the DPDP Act governs transfers of personal data outside India (source: https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework), and RBI rules require payment system data to be stored in India (source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11244). Involve NCS for government or CII scopes.

## "Who is liable if there is a PDPA breach?"

- The customer keeps its own obligations under the PDPA as the organisation responsible for the data (source: https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act).
- Singtel's responsibilities are set by the contract and its data processing terms. The account executive never promises liability caps or indemnities; route the question to Legal through the deal room.
- Quote PDPA penalty amounts only from the PDPC source, never from memory.
- Position controls that reduce exposure: Unified SASE and Managed Security Services, and Quantum-Safe Network for long-lived sensitive data.

## "Your invoices do not match our new Oracle system"

- Acknowledge it and fix it before selling. A disputed invoice drops Paper Process to 0.
- Agree the Oracle supplier site code, one PO line per site, service credits as negative invoice lines, and one named billing contact on each side.
- Record the fix in a separate Chatter thread tagged with the invoice ID, such as SGT-INV-2026-8841.
- Never trade a billing fix for a commercial concession.

## "We already buy cloud from a hyperscaler reseller"

- Position RE:AI, Nxera, and CUBΣ as complementary: sovereign hosting for regulated workloads, and CUBΣ for visibility and control over the customer's digital infrastructure (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/connectivity/cube).
