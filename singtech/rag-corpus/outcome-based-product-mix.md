# Outcome-based product mix

Map each operational gap to an outcome bundle. Skip products the customer already owns; for owned products, propose an uplift instead of a new line. Indicative ARR is the sum of catalogue ARR for the products in the bundle; it is never Singtel list pricing.

## Unified SASE & SD-WAN + 5G

- Products: Cyber Security (SGT-CYBER), CUBΣ (SGT-CUBE), 5G+ (SGT-5G).
- What it delivers: Unified SASE convergence for edge-to-cloud connectivity and Managed Security Services (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/cybersecurity); one platform for enterprise networking including SD-WAN, network management, security, data, and AI (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/connectivity/cube); 5G with network slicing (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/5g).
- Gap signals: several sites with local internet breakout, partner tails, Wi-Fi congestion, or no single security policy.
- Indicative ARR: SGD 370,000.

## Private 5G + Edge

- Products: 5G+ (SGT-5G) with multi-access edge computing.
- What it delivers: low-latency applications processed at the edge, orchestrated through Paragon (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/5g).
- Gap signals: government, defence, or CII data residency terms; cranes, AGVs, or apron operations that need on-site processing.
- Indicative ARR: SGD 140,000.

## RE:AI sovereign AI + hyperscaler orchestration

- Products: RE:AI (SGT-REAI), Nxera (SGT-NXERA), CUBΣ (SGT-CUBE).
- What it delivers: GPU-as-a-Service on NVIDIA DGX GB300 and token-based AI services hosted in Singapore (source: https://www.re-ai.ai/about-us/newsroom/singtel-re-ai-first-in-APAC-to-deploy-nvidia-dgx-gb300); AI-ready colocation at DC Tuas with direct-to-chip liquid cooling (source: https://www.itnews.asia/news/singtels-nxera-opens-58mw-data-centre-in-singapore-623491); CUBΣ for visibility and control over the customer's digital infrastructure (source: https://www.singtel.com/business/products-services/connectivity/cube).
- Gap signals: GPU queues, AI models calling overseas endpoints, or contract terms that require in-country processing.
- Indicative ARR: SGD 620,000.

## Sequencing

1. Fix the pain with the highest measured cost first.
2. Offer a starter bundle when budget or the Economic Buyer is not confirmed.
3. Tie each bundle to one metric the customer named in MEDDPICC.
