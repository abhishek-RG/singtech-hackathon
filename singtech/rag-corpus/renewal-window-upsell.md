# Renewal-window upsell motion

The upsell window opens 18 months before contract end and closes 12 months before it (365 to 548 days before the end date, inclusive). SingTech flags accounts inside this window.

## Why this window

Inside 12 months, the customer is in renewal mode: procurement runs a competitive check and the conversation turns to price. Between 12 and 18 months out, budget for the next cycle is still being planned, so an outcome-based bundle can be built into it.

## Motion

1. 18 months out: refresh the account plan. Confirm owned products, open incidents, billing status, and site feasibility.
2. 15 to 18 months out: run a value review with the economic buyer, using measured strain (link load, sync times, incidents) as evidence.
3. 12 to 15 months out: submit an outcome-based proposal with indicative ARR derived from catalogue ARR, plus a starter option for a smaller first step.
4. Inside 12 months: renewal defence. Clear disputed or overdue invoices, close SLA root causes, and lock the renewal before a competitive check.
5. 90 days before the end date: SingTech raises a renewal alert and drafts a Salesforce Task for the account executive to approve.

## Inputs SingTech uses

Contract end date, MEDDPICC total, open SLA incidents and credits, disputed or overdue invoices, and competitor activity.

## Rules

- Do not discount to win a renewal. Offer the starter bundle instead.
- Resolve billing disputes before the proposal reaches legal.
